Privacy Policy

DiscoverDE – German learning app  ·  Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy explains what information the DiscoverDE app (the “app”) handles, why, and what choices you have. We have kept the app simple on purpose: there are no user accounts, and we do not run analytics or tracking tools of our own.

At a glance

  • No account and no registration. The app never asks for your name or email address.
  • Your progress stays on your device. Saved words, favourites, bookmarks, completed stories and settings are stored locally.
  • Content is downloaded from our servers. Stories, audio, books and quizzes are loaded from GitHub Pages.
  • Ads are shown by Google AdMob, which may use your device’s advertising ID.
  • Optional AI features send what you enter (a word, or a city and level) to Google’s Gemini API.
  • The complaint form emails your message to us, only when you press send.

2. Information stored on your device

The app keeps the following only in its private storage on your device. We do not receive a copy of it.

  • Your chosen interface language.
  • Words you saved, and words and stories you marked as favourites or bookmarked.
  • Which stories you have completed, and your badges and progress.
  • Display and audio settings, such as night mode and your chosen text-to-speech voice.
  • Stories, audio files and books you downloaded for offline use, and the date you last read the news.
  • Stories created with the AI story generator, so you can read them again later. Words found with the AI word lookup are stored only if you choose to save them.

4. Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google may use your device’s advertising ID and other technical data, and may show ads that are personalised. Google acts as an independent controller for this processing; see Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses data in advertising.

You can limit this at any time in your device settings: on Android, open Settings → Privacy → Ads (on some devices Settings → Google → Ads) to opt out of ads personalisation or reset your advertising ID.

5. AI features (Google Gemini)

When you use the AI story generator or the AI word lookup, the app sends your request to Google’s Gemini API and receives text back. Google processes this data under its Gemini API terms.

Please do not type personal information, such as names, addresses or contact details, into the word lookup. The request is not linked to an account because the app has none.

6. Complaint form

If you spot a mistake in a story, word, book or audio file, you can report it with the complaint form. Pressing send transmits the text you wrote and the title of the item to us by email through Mailgun. The app does not add your name, email address or device identifiers to this message. Mailgun and the mail providers involved will see technical connection data such as your IP address.

Nothing is sent unless you press send. Please do not include personal information in your message. If you want us to reply, you will need to include your own contact details.

7. Device permissions and features

  • Internet access is needed to load content, show ads and use the AI features.
  • Storage is used inside the app’s private area for downloaded stories, audio and books.
  • Microphone: the app does not record or listen to audio. Audio playback is limited to the stories and books you choose to play.
  • Text-to-speech uses the speech engine installed on your device. Depending on your device and its settings, that engine may be provided by your device manufacturer or Google.
  • The app does not use your camera, location, contacts or photos.

8. Legal bases (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we rely on the following:

  • Delivering the app and its content (technical connection data): our legitimate interest in providing the app, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.
  • AI features and the complaint form: the data is processed because you ask us to, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, and, for complaints, our legitimate interest in correcting content, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.
  • Ads and advertising identifiers: your consent where the law requires it, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG; otherwise our legitimate interest in funding the free app, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

9. How long data is kept

Data stored on your device stays there until you delete it. You can remove everything at any time by clearing the app’s storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

Complaint messages are kept for as long as needed to review and fix the reported content, and then deleted. Data held by Google, GitHub and Mailgun is kept according to their own policies.

10. Transfers outside your country

Google, GitHub and Mailgun may process data in the United States and other countries. Where this applies, those providers rely on safeguards such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses. Please see their privacy policies for details.

11. Your rights

Under data protection law you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or export your personal data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example in the country where you live or work, or where the controller is based.

Because the app has no accounts, we hold almost no data that identifies you. The main exception is a complaint message you send us. To exercise your rights regarding that, email us at discover.me.de@gmail.com. For data held by Google, GitHub, Mailgun or PayPal, please contact those companies directly.

12. Children

The app is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Security

All connections the app makes use HTTPS. Data on your device is kept in the app’s private storage, which other apps cannot normally read. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

14. Links to other sites

The app can open external pages, for example PayPal or the source pages of books. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those sites.
